Our History

Hebron Enterprises started its operations in Coimbatore in 1991, aiming to bridge the gap between heavy pump manufacturers and local businesses, builders, and farmers. Recognizing the high demand for reliable water pumping technologies, we partner with C.R.I. Pumps—a brand synonymous with outstanding engineering and premium quality.

Over the past decades, we have supplied and serviced thousands of pumps across Tamil Nadu. Our clients trust us because we do not just sell a product; we provide a complete engineering solution based on precise calculations of head range, discharge requirements, friction loss, and local grid voltages.

Today, Hebron Enterprises is recognized as one of the most reliable water equipment dealers in Coimbatore, providing customized systems for overhead tanks, deep borewells, multi-story commercial buildings, and off-grid solar-powered agriculture fields.
